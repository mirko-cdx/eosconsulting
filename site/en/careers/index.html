Careers

A career at EOS requires common sense, sharp thinking, ideas and the ambition to exceed our clients' high expectations time and again. An MBA, a doctorate or certifications are not prerequisites for starting out and advancing at EOS.

The academic and personal development of our consultants creates substance in our company. That is why we specifically support you in your further education plans.

Absolute focus on what matters

Creating substance also means consistently saying no. We deliberately distance ourselves from overheads and rigid structures and concentrate on what matters: consulting.

EOS supports young careers

We support young careers as early as university and offer motivated high potentials the opportunity to gain their first consulting experience with us. Whether as part of an internship, a project or a thesis, we are open to everything. All that matters is that you convince us of yourself and your topic.

 

Graduates are welcome too! In addition to exciting projects at technology-driven companies, we offer extensive opportunities for further training and a mentoring programme.
