A defined procedure

The timeline of a project at EOS is known and communicated before the project starts. This approach is results-focused, and reporting and the target/actual comparison are aligned directly with this process.

Contact phase

In the contact phase, you have a conversation with a manager or a very experienced consultant from EOS. The aim is to exchange ideas and identify potential synergies.

If you are specifically interested, you can contact us directly here.

Definition phase

Once we have identified initial starting points for a joint project, the next step is to formulate concrete expectations and goals and to define success factors. In addition to an EOS manager, an experienced consultant will support you.

Execution phase

After a project has been successfully defined and launched, our consultants support you throughout the project. Status meetings are held at regular intervals to review the goals. Here, the work delivered is confirmed or adjustments are made. In addition to the consultant team, a manager takes part to ensure quality.

Closing phase

Project results must be usable for the client. That is why, at the close of a project, we record the successes achieved and the lessons learned. You also receive a recommendation on the most effective use and communication of the project results.

The framework for your process environment

We see this structure as the framework for our projects. If your organisation uses its own standards for projects and structures, this framework can be extended with suitable interfaces so that the benefits of projects are integrated into day-to-day work.

 

We would be glad to discuss potential synergies.
