Privacy policy

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or tracking tools and does not embed external fonts or videos. External content (Calendly) is only loaded if you explicitly click to load it.

This English version is provided for convenience. The German version is legally binding.

1. Controller

eosconsulting GmbH, represented by its managing director Mirko Haucke
Turmgasse 12, 69181 Leimen, Germany
Email: haucke@eosconsulting.de

2. Hosting

This website is operated on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, in a data centre in the European Union. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Hetzner.

When you access the website, the web server processes technically necessary data transmitted by your browser, in particular your IP address, date and time, the page requested, browser type and referrer. This data is processed only to deliver the page and to secure operation. Access logs are not stored. Server error messages may contain IP addresses; they are used only for troubleshooting and are deleted regularly. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in the secure and error-free operation of the website.

3. Contact form, email and phone

The contact form does not transmit any data to our server. When you submit it, your email program opens with a prepared message that you send yourself. If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our emails are processed via Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd., Dublin). We delete your data as soon as it is no longer required and no statutory retention obligations apply.

4. Appointment booking with Calendly

On the contact page you can book an appointment via the Calendly service, provided by Calendly LLC, USA. The booking calendar is only loaded when you click "Load booking calendar". Only then does your browser connect to Calendly; in particular your IP address and technical data are transmitted, and Calendly may use cookies or similar technologies. If you book an appointment, Calendly also processes the data you enter (e.g. name, email address, selected time) and passes it on to us.

The legal basis is your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG, which you give by clicking; for carrying out a booked appointment also Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Calendly is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision. Alternatively, you can always contact us by email or phone.

5. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future. To do so, simply contact us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.

6. SSL/TLS encryption

This website is delivered exclusively via encrypted HTTPS connections.
