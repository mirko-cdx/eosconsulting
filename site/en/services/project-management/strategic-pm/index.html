Communication in teams

Regular communication is the heartbeat of projects. Results are achieved and communicated in project team meetings, status meetings and task forces. The danger lies in over-communication: resources get tied up, the day consists only of meetings and substantive work stalls. A sense of proportion is what matters here.

Learn more.

Personality

Especially in crises and in the hotter phases of a project, it is important that the project manager has a balanced personality in order to recognise opportunities, evaluate alternatives objectively and make decisions that lead to the goal. EOS sets the highest standards for its consultants here.
