Who are we?

EOS is a consulting company that specialises in four core services in complex, technical environments: project management, process management, quality management and R&D controlling.

 

Our clients are technology- and process-driven companies that shape the future by building on existing knowledge.

Focus on the client

Everything we do has one goal: to exceed our clients' expectations. To achieve this, we rely on the best consultants in the industry and develop solutions tailored to our clients.

Our consultants are always happy to go the extra mile and deliver projects under difficult conditions just as our clients wish.

Learn more.

Positioning

We see ourselves as a company positioned between strategy consultants and technology consultants. This means that we deliver strategic projects without losing sight of the operational side. It also ensures that in operational projects we remain aware of the strategic goals and align our actions accordingly.

Learn more.

Focus on our people

To deliver top performance consistently, day after day, people need the right environment. We deliberately foster this environment in our company and often break new ground in doing so. Our consultants are so successful because we discuss professional and personal goals together and then consistently put them into practice.

Learn more.

EOS spirit

Excellent concepts and methods are not everything. What matters is putting them into practice, and that can only be done by the right personalities. This is where the EOS spirit comes in: our consultants work highly creatively in teams and complement each other so that every individual's strengths can unfold. The result is outcomes that our clients actually live.

Understand - Act - Convince

We live our guiding motto "Understand - Act - Convince" every day for our clients.

 

Understand

Before we start a project, we make sure together with our clients that we are on the same page and have truly understood the problem.

 

Act

Our actions are aligned with the goals we agree on with our clients. The direction and objectives of a project may change; what matters is continuous alignment so that we do not head in the wrong direction or lose sight of the actual goals.

 

Convince

By "understanding", we lay a solid foundation for our clients' project success. Our "acting" builds a stable framework on this foundation within the project environment.

 

The results of our work convince our clients!
