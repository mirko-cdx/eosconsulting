Processes as part of the corporate culture

Processes are often mystified and communicated as an ideal. Yet they should be an integral part of the corporate culture and the link between employees and value-adding activities. To achieve this, EOS brings its full range of knowledge from different industries, personalities and methods.

Understanding processes

Processes are an ever-present topic, and their benefits are often presented convincingly. But how many processes, and how much of the formality that comes with them, does your company really need? The EOS philosophy is: as much as necessary to move your product and your company forward. Our consultants like to draw on experience from other industries to find this optimum.

Learn more.

Introducing processes

Defining and introducing processes is often a political matter. The key decision-makers must be involved, and subject-matter experts must be convinced of the workflow and its added value.

The challenge is to strike a balance between a great deal of tact and a clear, consistent line. EOS consultants know the most common forms of resistance to change and can deal with them in a targeted way.

Learn more.

Optimising processes

Many companies already use good processes. But day-to-day challenges often require excellent processes. By quickly understanding your current process landscape, EOS can identify specific potential for improvement, address it in a politically neutral way from an external perspective, and formulate and implement proposals for improvement.

Learn more.

Living processes

What else you should know...

Process topics often become urgent in response to specific events, such as M&A activities, cost-saving programmes, restructuring, realignment of the product portfolio and more. Here it is essential to feed the causes and effects of the event precisely into the processes and to involve top management. EOS offers highly specialised solutions for these challenges.
